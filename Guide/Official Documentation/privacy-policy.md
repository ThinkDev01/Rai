# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [effective date]

This Privacy Policy explains how **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("Rai", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use Rai, our project and sprint management platform available at [product URL] (the "Service").

We act as a **Data Fiduciary** for the account and usage data we collect directly. For the content you and your team put into an Organization (your "Customer Data"), your Organization is the controller and we process that data on its behalf.

This policy is aligned with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**.

---

## 1. Data we collect

**Account data** — when you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and authentication details. If you sign in via Google or Microsoft, we receive basic profile information from that provider.

**Organization data** — the organizations you create or join, your role, and your membership relationships.

**Customer Data** — content you and your team create within the Service: projects, sprints, tickets, comments, @mentions, and similar. This may include personal data about your colleagues that your Organization chooses to enter.

**Payment data** — when you subscribe, payments are processed by **Razorpay**. We receive confirmation and limited billing metadata (such as transaction status), but **we do not store your full card or bank details** — those are handled by Razorpay.

**Usage and technical data** — log data such as IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, and actions taken in the Service. We use this to operate, secure, and improve the platform.

---

## 2. How we use your data

We use personal data to:

- Provide, maintain, and secure the Service and your account.
- Authenticate you and manage organization access and roles.
- Process subscriptions and payments.
- Send service-related communications — invites, notifications, billing alerts, and important account or security notices.
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
- Monitor, troubleshoot, and improve performance, reliability, and security.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

---

## 3. Legal basis and consent

We process personal data on the basis of:

- **Your consent**, given when you create an account and use the Service.
- **Performance of a contract** — to deliver the Service you have signed up for.
- **Legitimate and lawful uses** — such as securing the platform and complying with law, as permitted under the DPDP Act.

You may withdraw consent at any time (see *Your rights* below); doing so may limit your ability to use the Service.

## 4. How we share data

We share personal data only as needed to run the Service:

- **Service providers (Data Processors)** acting on our instructions:
  - **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** — cloud hosting and infrastructure, identity and authentication (**AWS Cognito**), and transactional email (**AWS SES**).
  - **Razorpay** — payment processing.
  - **Google / Microsoft** — only if you choose to sign in through them.
- **Within your Organization** — your name, email, role, and the Customer Data you create are visible to other members of your Organization according to its permissions.
- **Legal and safety** — where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the Service.
- **Business transfers** — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, data may be transferred, subject to this policy.

We require our processors to protect personal data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

## 5. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account or Organization is active, and as needed to provide the Service. After account or Organization closure, we retain data for a reasonable period to allow for recovery, then delete or anonymize it, except where longer retention is required by law (for example, financial and tax records). You may request earlier deletion as described below.

## 6. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect personal data, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) across the Service.
- Authentication and account security managed by **AWS Cognito**.
- Strict per-Organization data isolation.
- Access controls limiting internal access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work continuously to safeguard your data and will notify affected users and authorities of a personal data breach as required by law.

---

## 7. International data transfers

Our infrastructure runs on AWS. Where personal data is processed or stored outside India, we take steps to ensure it is handled in line with applicable law and this policy.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct or update** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Withdraw consent** to processing.
- **Grievance redressal** — raise a complaint about how your data is handled.
- **Nominate** another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **support@rai.novetum.com**. Some data (such as data within an Organization you belong to) may need to be addressed through your Organization's administrators, who control that workspace.
